Rooms that exist only while you're in them

Read before you join

  • Everyone here learns your IP address. Direct connections are what keep the server out of your conversation; the cost is that peers see each other's addresses. Encryption does not change this.
  • Whoever opens the room sets its size and hosts it. Eight is the default because every peer sends audio to every other peer, and voice gets unstable past that. The host can change it, up to 16.
  • Nothing is saved anywhere. No account, no history, no server-side copy. Close the tab and the room is gone, including for everyone still reading it.
